Goal Book side panel

Simulation · pages shortened · the agent's choices are placeholders

A beginner learning AWS searches and reads the real documentation. The agent lives in the browser's side panel: it suggests where to look, marks results, keeps a map of the page, points to the part to read, and takes what the learner finds into the book. Everything the agent adds to a page carries the Goal Book mark, so the learner always knows which parts are the site and which are the agent.
